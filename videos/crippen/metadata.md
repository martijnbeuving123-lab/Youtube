## Description

In July 1910, a ship's captain in the middle of the Atlantic sent a 29-word wireless message to Scotland Yard. It is widely described as the first time wireless telegraphy helped to catch a man wanted for murder.

This is the full story of the Crippen case: the disappearance of the music hall singer Cora Crippen (Belle Elmore) from 39 Hilldrop Crescent, the discovery in the cellar, the flight of Hawley Harvey Crippen and Ethel Le Neve aboard the SS Montrose, Chief Inspector Walter Dew's race across the ocean on the faster SS Laurentic, the arrest at Father Point, and the trial at the Old Bailey. It also covers the 2007 DNA claim and why the conviction still stands.
