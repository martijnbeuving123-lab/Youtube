## Description

Three women died in their baths in three different towns between 1912 and 1914. Every death was ruled an accident. All three had married the same man, under three different names.

This is the full story of the Brides in the Bath case: George Joseph Smith's string of bigamous marriages, the deaths of Bessie Mundy in Herne Bay, Alice Burnham in Blackpool and Margaret Lofty in Highgate, the letter from Blackpool that linked them, Inspector Arthur Neil's investigation, Bernard Spilsbury's reconstruction with the baths themselves, and the 1915 Old Bailey trial in which the jury was allowed to hear about all three deaths as evidence of a "system".

Smith was tried for, and convicted of, the murder of Bessie Mundy. The other two deaths were put before the jury as similar-fact evidence. Where the record is uncertain, this video says so.
