## Description

Monday, 27 March 1905. Thomas and Ann Farrow are found beaten in their paint shop at 34 Deptford High Street, south-east London. The cash box is empty. The only clue is a greasy smudge on its inner tray: a thumbprint.

This is the full story of the Deptford murders, also known as the Mask Murders or the Farrow murders: Melville Macnaghten's handkerchief, Inspector Charles Collins and Scotland Yard's four-year-old Fingerprint Bureau, the arrest of the brothers Alfred and Albert Stratton, and the Old Bailey trial in which a defence expert was undone by two letters written on the same day. It became the first murder conviction in Britain in which fingerprint evidence was central.

Where the record is uncertain, this video says so. Some details, such as the exact sum taken, are reported differently in different accounts.
