# Originality rules — both channels

Why: YouTube reviews every Partner Program application by hand and rejects channels whose videos
look mass-produced, templated or repetitive ("inauthentic content") or that mostly repackage
other people's material ("reused content"). The owner's decision (2026-11-27): every video must
pass these rules from day one, so a year of work is not rejected at the review. The daily
production runs apply them before rendering; the weekly review audits them (step 3f).

## Every video must have
1. **Its own angle.** One sentence in `script.md`: what this video argues or shows that other
   videos on the case do not (a forensic detail, a mistake in the investigation, a myth
   corrected, a link to another case). Not just "the story of X".
2. **Analysis, not only narration.** At least two passages where the narrator weighs evidence,
   compares sources or says what historians disagree about, clearly marked as interpretation.
3. **A different opening.** The first 30 seconds may not reuse the structure or phrases of the
   channel's last 10 videos (compare their `script.md` openings). Rotate hook types.
4. **Varied, transformed visuals.** At least four kinds: archive photos, documents with close-ups
   or highlights, maps, timelines or diagrams made by us, newspaper headlines, location
   images. No single image on screen longer than about 20 seconds. Our own annotations, crops
   and graphics on archive material.
5. **No boilerplate.** No fixed intro or outro phrases repeated word for word across videos
   (one short channel sign-off is fine). Titles and thumbnails follow the house style but their
   words are specific to the case.
6. **Visible research.** Sources with links in the description; contested points said as such
   in the narration.
7. **Honest labelling.** AI disclosure Yes; never present a generic image as the specific
   person, place or document.

## Gate before render (daily run)
Check rules 1–6 against `script.md`, `beats.json` and the image list. If one fails, fix the
script or visuals first. Record the result in one line in the video's `metadata.md`
("Originality gate: passed — angle: …"). Never skip the gate to make the deadline; a later
upload is better than a templated one.

## What only the owner can decide (proposals, not rules)
- A human voice (owner or freelancer) for the most important videos once a channel approaches
  the threshold: the strongest single protection against an "inauthentic" verdict.
- Making the old short "Part x of 3" videos private before applying, if the audit says they
  weaken the channel.
- Slowing to 4–5 videos a week if quality cannot be held at daily cadence.
